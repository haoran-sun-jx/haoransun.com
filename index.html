Haoran Sun

Postdoc Research Fellow at Durham University

I am a Research Fellow at Durham University. I received my Ph.D in Economics from Durham University in 2023. My research interests are in applied microeconomics, with a focus on innovation, productivity, and taxation. My work mainly focuses on policy evaluation for the firm-level productivity and growth of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the UK.
